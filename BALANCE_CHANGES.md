# Balance Changes

Every change OpenTM2 makes to how Twisted Metal 2 plays. They're always on online, so everyone plays by the same
rules. In local play each one can be switched off in Options › Vanilla Plus, or all of them with the Original preset.

The newest update is at the top.

---

## v1.0.17 (November 26, 2026)

### General (V1.0.17)

- **Shots through pickups:** ricochet bombs, missiles, bullets, and Dark Tooth's swoop pass through pickups. Before, a ricochet bomb could get trapped bouncing between a pickup and a wall, missiles and bullets stopped at the Los Angeles and Moscow bonus pickups, and Dark Tooth's swoop ended at the first pickup it touched.
- **Mines:** a mine is removed 15 seconds after its owner is destroyed. Before, mines stayed for good. The game type's Mine timer setting (Energy attacks) can remove every mine 15 seconds after it's laid instead.
- **Players' wrecks:** a destroyed player's wreck burns, spins, and is blown apart 22 seconds later, like a computer car's, unless the player comes back first. Before, it stayed in the arena for good, solid and hard to see, stopping shots and ramming cars that touched it.

## v1.0.14 (November 24, 2026)

### General (V1.0.14)

- **Machine guns:** bullets fly 25% farther before they fade, as in the PC version.

### Characters (V1.0.14)

#### Grasshopper

- **Special damage:** 18 → 20.

#### Thumper

- **Special:** can no longer be fired again while his flame is still burning. Before, the second one cost the energy and did nothing.

## v1.0.0 (November 20, 2026)

### General (V1.0.0)

- **Fire missile:** 7 → 10 damage.
- **Homing missile:** 10 → 7 damage.
- **Machine guns:** every bullet that connects now does half damage, instead of a 50% chance of full damage. The same damage on average, without the luck.
- **Shield:** casting a shield while one is already up no longer wastes the energy without giving another shield.
- **Mines:** mines no longer expire. They stay until something drives over them, up to 5 per driver (a 6th removes the oldest).
- **Freeze:** freezing a car again while it's still frozen, or within 1 second after it thaws, now freezes it for half as long each time, so a car can't be kept frozen.
- **Ricochet bomb:** no longer hits its own car right after launch.
- **Remote bomb:** blasts on slanted surfaces no longer do extra damage (25 at most), the same bug as Shadow's.
- **Pickup respawns:** every 10 seconds a taken pickup now always comes back. Before, the game sometimes picked one that was still there, so nothing came back.
- **Antarctica:** the fire missile on the ice floes can now be picked up.
- **Computer cars:** no longer jam against other cars and pile up; they back out and take another way.

### Characters (V1.0.0)

#### Roadkill

- **Boomerang:** no longer vanishes next to him; it always gives its charge back when it returns.

#### Twister

- **Special recharge:** 18 → 30 seconds.
- **Tornado:** no weapons while it spins, and she takes no ram damage during it.
- **Tornado's throw:** the cars still caught are now thrown out when the tornado ends (it never happened before, a bug).

#### Axel

- **Rams:** 100% → 110% damage.
- **Crush:** 15 → 0 damage. His rams no longer crush for free (essentially giving Axel unlimited free Hammerhead specials).

#### Mr. Slam

- **Arm swing:** Mr. Slam can't use additional weapons until the swing ends. His shield and machine guns still work.

#### Shadow

- **Special:** no longer does extra damage on slanted surfaces (a bug).
- **Special damage:** 25 → 28, to compensate.

#### Hammerhead

- **Rams:** 100% → 120% damage.
- **Crush:** 15 → 20 damage.
- **Special recharge:** 60 → 35 seconds.
- **Brakes:** 1 → 2.
- **Grip speed:** 25 → 60.

#### Outlaw 2

- **Special recharge:** 55 → 45 seconds.
- **Taser:** a shot that missed can still connect while it lasts (one hit of 10, instead of 2 totaling 20).

#### Mr. Grimm

- **Special recharge:** 70 → 60 seconds.

#### Grasshopper

- **Special damage:** 15 → 18.
- **Special:** now pushes the car it hits.
- **Leap:** she takes no ram damage while leaping, and lands cleaner.
- **Energy jump:** 120 → 0 energy.

#### Thumper

- **Special recharge:** 80 → 75 seconds.

#### Minion

- **Rams:** 100% → 110% damage.
- **Crush:** 15 → 0 damage. His rams no longer crush for free (essentially giving Minion unlimited free Hammerhead specials).
- **Special recharge:** 18 → 30 seconds.
