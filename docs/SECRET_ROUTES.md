# The secret arenas with computer cars

Suicide Swamp (game level 10), Cyburbia (11) and Roof Tops (12) were 2-player only in the original game, and their
computer-car route data was never finished (decomp `SECRET_LEVELS.md`). The port lets them be picked in custom
matches and Survival once unlocked (2, 3 and 4 drivers' endings, everyone signed in counted), after Hong Kong in the
Arena row; the match keeps the choice 9..11 and `arena_level` (ui/options.c) gives the game's level.

## Step 1 (made 2026-10-15): the loaded data fixed

`src/vplus/secret_routes.c` wraps each arena's loader and fixes the data after it:

| Arena | Route points | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Suicide Swamp | 8 (one chain along the L: west field, bridge, corridor, east strip) | section bytes 0,0,0,0,0,1,1,1; the field and the strip joined through the corridor (next node 4 / 5) |
| Cyburbia | 10 (the start points) | section bytes 1,1,1,1,1,1,0,0,0,1 |
| Roof Tops | 9 (the start points) | section bytes 11,10,8,8,8,8,0,3,6; the table's ~49 lost points replaced by -1 (a straight chase) |

Seen in the `secret` scenario (`TM2PC_TEST_ARENA=10..12`, 2 players and 6 computer cars, 2.5 minutes): every arena
plays a full match with kills spread over the field; no car fell on Roof Tops in that run.

## Step 2: Cyburbia's own route

`src/vplus/cyburbia_route.inc` (generated by `tools/routes/cyburbia.py`; edit the script, not the file) replaces
the loaded route after `arenaLoadCyburbia`:

- **127 route points** (the game's limit is 140) over the two street grids, the ring road and the plaza, each with up to 4
  links and a lane byte per link (its top speed: -2 a quarter, -1 a third, 0 half, 1 two thirds, 2 full). The
  Singletrac mod's 173-point route was the rough draft (where the roads are). Changes from it:
  - its parked and unreachable points and doubled trunks dropped;
  - one junction per fork;
  - corners thinned;
  - its one-way slips made two-way;
  - points on walls and tunnel mouths moved off them.
  The plaza's two north entries stay one-way, as in the mod.
- **35 sections**, the game's limit. Section 0 is retail's plaza box, with its surfaces 0..39. The other 34 are 24
  street rectangles and 10 ring pieces, each with retail's section-1 ground (floor 0, surfaces 40..45), so the cars'
  wheels behave as before. Our own next-node / next-section tables join them.
- **15 start points** (points 0..14), none in a tunnel. Point 0 is not in the plaza: Dark Tooth's Hong Kong warp can
  fire at point 0 on any level.

How the computer cars use it (decomp `aiFollowRouteTarget`, `aiPickLinkNode`):
- In the target's section, a car chases the target.
- Otherwise it drives to the exit point `next[mine][theirs]`, then on into `next_section`.
- At each point it takes the link nearest where it is going, so every section is laid out so that heading for any of
  its points that way works.

Checked offline with a simulation of that walk: every one of the 16,002 point-to-point trips arrives. The median
trip is the shortest path; 90% are within 1.21 times it.

In game (the `sixteen` scenario on arena 11 with `TM2PC_AI_TRACE`, 14 computer cars, about a minute): 30 of the
35 sections reached, 13 stalls (6450 VBlanks in all) against retail New York's 17 (10740), mean speed 575 against 379.
The plaza's south-east entry link was removed: its chute throws cars into a walled pocket.

## Step 3: Roof Tops' own route

`src/vplus/roof_tops_route.inc` (generated by `tools/routes/roof_tops.py`, which reads the disc's extracted
`TERRAIN` folder given in `TM2_TERRAIN`) replaces the loaded route after `arenaLoadRoofTops`:

- **52 route points** on the 13 roofs. Retail's 9 start points stay points 0..8; two are moved in from edges:
  - 0 sat 720 from F's south edge;
  - 7 sat at the turn into narrow Q.
  Six more start points are spread over the roofs, 15 in all.
- **Jumps as on New York:**
  - a run-up point about 900 before the edge;
  - the landing point about 850 past the far edge;
  - the speed set by the speed table (`GRP+0x1281`, [from section][to section]) from the takeoff point.
- **Retail's 14 sections** are kept as they are: boxes, floors, flags and surfaces. Our next-node / next-section
  tables cover the 13 roofs, and 19 speed-table entries change:
  - 0 on same-height crossings;
  - 30 on drops onto a lower roof;
  - 80 on two jumps that land close to a wall;
  - 120 for the A east → E jump;
  - 40 up the B → Q ramp.
- **Targets in the street, the pit or the level under D:** retail's -1 (chase the target straight) is replaced by a
  hold on the car's own roof. Before, one car falling or jumping over a street pulled every car targeting it off
  the edge after it.
- **Points kept off edges:**
  - every point is at least 700 from an edge;
  - no dead-end points near one;
  - link speeds capped by how much roof is left past their end;
  - B's narrow west alley left out.

Game rules on Roof Tops (`secret_routes.c`):
- **From New York:** `aiShouldChaseDirect` and `aiNearSectionEdge` run as on New York (the level read as 5 during
  the call): a straight chase only at a target on the car's own roof, and braking near edges.
- **Melee cars** (a ram or grab special):
  - New York's branch never clears their straight chase, and a landed special sets it with no section test;
  - on Roof Tops it holds only while the target is on the car's own roof, and a special's follow-through ends when
    the target is on another roof (`aiMovementUpdate` wrapped).

In game, about a minute with `TM2PC_AI_TRACE`:

| Test | Falls | Stalls (VBlanks) | Mean speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Roof Tops, 16 cars | 3 | 4 (1560) | 417 |
| Retail New York, 16 cars | 4 | 17 (10740) | 379 |
| Roof Tops, 6 cars | 1 | 7 (4080) | 414 |
| Retail New York, 6 cars | 0 | 12 (6870) | 337 |

The falls left happen in fights, off the route: cars backing up, knocked off by a hit, or chasing a target on the
same roof past its edge, as on New York.

## Next

- **Cyburbia, in-game tuning.** Things to watch:
  - cars circling at the fork junctions;
  - cars bunching at the one-way north entries (they may work two-way);
  - the ring's tunnel mouths.
- **Roof Tops, things to watch:**
  - the P → C and D → A jumps at speed 80;
  - the narrow ramps;
  - cars in the pit: retail's way out may be blocked by a wall panel, and if it is, a car there needs a respawn;
  - a car backing up off an edge. Data can't stop it; a rule could stop reversing near a lethal edge.
- **Suicide Swamp:** more points only if cars get stuck at the bridge or the corridor.
- Optional: cap the AI's level-based strength at Hong Kong's (8) on these arenas.

Tools:
- `TM2PC_AI_TRACE=N` logs each computer car every N VBlanks: place, section, route points, health and target.

For mods: a level needs its own route points for computer cars to drive it; 140 points at most (the game's array)
and 35 sections.
