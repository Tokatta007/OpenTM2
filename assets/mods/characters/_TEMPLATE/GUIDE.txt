Characters: a new driver and car
================================

A character is a new car in car select, with its own name, driver portraits, model and textures. It sits at the
end of the Left / Right order, after every regular car (after Spectre, and after Sweet Tooth, Minion and Dark Tooth
when they are unlocked or at the end).

Every character is built on a base car, one of the game's 15. The base gives everything the character does not set
itself: its special weapon's behavior, its sounds when it brings none (see "Sounds"), and every number left out of
[character] and [ai] (see "Stats" and "The computer driver").

Computer cars drive characters too: pick one as a Challenge Match opponent, or let Random pick one in Challenge
Match, Custom Tournament and Survival (Options > Mods, "Mod Cars in Random"). The game's own Tournament keeps its
drivers.


Making one
----------
1. Run tm2pc.exe --new-mod character crimson-fury: it makes mods\characters\crimson-fury with a mod.ini, its id and
   a new uid filled in. (Or copy this _TEMPLATE folder there and make a uid yourself: ../../README.txt.)
2. Edit mod.ini: the character's name, your name, and its base car (see "The cars" below).
3. Add the files below.
4. Run tm2pc.exe --check-mod mods\characters\crimson-fury until it prints OK.
5. Start the game. In car select, press Left from Roadkill (or go Right past the last car): your character is there.
   Options > Mods turns each character on or off.


Files
-----
    mod.ini          the manifest (../../README.txt) and [character] base = <the base car>
    car.dmd          the car's model, in the game's own format (as the disc's DMD\<code>.DMD)
    car.tms          the car's textures, in the game's own format (as the disc's TMS\<code>.TMS)
    portrait_1.bmp   the driver's portrait in car select: 64 x 128, or larger in the same shape (128 x 256 ...)
    portrait_2.bmp   the same once the car is chosen (the game shows a second portrait)
    carinfo.bmp      the CAR INFO screen: 640 x 480, the whole screen as a picture (its ratings and story are yours
                     to draw: make them match its stats)
    plate.bmp        the car's picture in the match line-up, the lobby and the opponents page: 128 x 64, or larger
                     in the same shape

Optional:
    carselect.dmd, carselect.tms     the car turning in car select, as a pair; without them car select turns
                     car.dmd / car.tms (the DMD at most 128 KB, the TMS at most 32 pictures)
    engine.wav       its engine (see "Sounds")
    special.wav      its special weapon's sound (see "Sounds")
    ending.txt       the story shown when it wins the tournament (see "The ending")
    ending.bmp       the ending's picture: 640 x 480, or 16:9 (see "The ending")
    ending.mpg       the ending's video: MPEG-1, at most 2 minutes (see "The video")

The name shown in car select and in matches is [mod] name. The car-select font has no round brackets: "(" and ")"
show as square ones.


The model
---------
The game finds parts of a car's model by number (the special weapon's objects, moving parts such as Axel's wheels or
Mr. Slam's arm, the damage looks), the numbers of the base car. Start from the base car's own DMD and change its
meshes, keeping its node tree and numbers, or those parts go missing. Pick the base whose special and moving parts
fit your car.

The hit box comes from the size numbers (see "Stats"), not from the model: when the model is bigger or smaller than
the base car, set track_width, the axles and the heights to match it.

A converter from common 3D formats is planned; until then a new model needs a tool that writes the game's DMD format.
The game's file codes: HH Hammerhead, OT Outlaw 2, WH Warthog, MG Mr. Grimm, PV Grasshopper, TH Thumper,
SP Spectre, RK Roadkill, IR Twister, AX Axel, FL Mr. Slam, HS Shadow, ST Sweet Tooth, MN Minion, BB Dark Tooth.


The cars
--------
[character] base = one of:
    Hammerhead, Outlaw 2, Warthog, Mr. Grimm, Grasshopper, Thumper, Spectre, Roadkill, Twister, Axel, Mr. Slam,
    Shadow, Sweet Tooth, Minion, Dark Tooth


Stats
-----
Each key under [character] is optional: one left out keeps the base car's number. The game's cars' own values are in
brackets, for comparison. A value outside its range is a problem (the mod is grayed in Options > Mods with the reason).

    top_speed = 80..150         (92 Sweet Tooth, Mr. Slam .. 141 Twister)
    acceleration = 3..12        (4 Mr. Slam .. 11 Mr. Grimm)
    brakes = 1..5               (1 Hammerhead .. 4 Thumper)
    turning = 60..110           the speed below which it turns less: lower turns tighter (65 .. 105)
    steering = 2..9             low-speed steering (3 .. 8)
    slide_speed = 20..150       above it the car slides (25 Hammerhead .. 150 Minion)
    armor = 50..300             max health (65 Mr. Grimm .. 300 Dark Tooth); a player gets 20 more, as every car
    mass = 5..90                weight in hits and rams (5 Mr. Grimm .. 60 Minion)

The size, which is also the hit box:
    track_width = 40..180       left to right wheels (50 Shadow .. 173 Dark Tooth); it also sets how easily the car
                                tips over, in proportion to the base car's
    front_axle = 30..230        front wheels ahead of the middle (41 .. 216)
    rear_axle = 30..280         rear wheels behind the middle (33 .. 269)
    ride_height = 8..120        the body above the ground (12 .. 116)
    roof_height = 24..110       the roof, when the car is upside down (32 .. 106)
    step_height = 40..160       the highest step it drives up (40 Mr. Grimm .. 160 Dark Tooth)

The special:
    special = own                   the base car's own special (the default), or another one to fire instead:
                                    a driver's: sweet_tooth, outlaw, warthog, mr_grimm, thumper, spectre, roadkill,
                                    axel, shadow, minion; or a pickup weapon's: fire_missile, homing_missile,
                                    remote_bomb, power_missile, napalm, ricochet, mega_napalm, freeze_missile.
                                    (Hammerhead's, Grasshopper's, Twister's, Mr. Slam's and Dark Tooth's need their own
                                    car: a character built on one of them keeps its own special.)
    special_recharge = 300..2400    frames for one more special (360 Sweet Tooth .. 1600 Thumper)
    special_max = 1..6              specials held at most (3 .. 5)
    special_damage = 0..8191        each shot's damage (also the taser's hits, the flame each frame, Shadow's strike)
    special_life = 1..8191          how long each shot flies, in frames (most 50; the spiral and boomerang 200)
    special_speed = 1..150          each shot's speed (150 is the game's limit for every shot)
    special_turn = 1..4095          how hard a homing shot turns
    special_range = 1..4000         the taser's reach (80) or Axel's shockwave radius (800)
    special_height = 1..4000        Axel's shockwave height (200)
    special_duration = 40..400      the taser's arc (40)
    special_freeze_damage = 0..8191 Minion's freeze missile's damage (7)
    special_freeze_time = 1..6000   how long its freeze missiles freeze, in ticks (300)
    special_burn_time = 1..2000     how long its flame or napalm burns a car, in frames (100)
    special_blast_damage = 0..8191  Shadow's blast at its center (25)
    special_blast_radius = 1..400   Shadow's blast radius (75)
    special_turn_back = 1..4000     how far the boomerang flies before it turns back (300)
    ai_special_ahead = 1..100       computer drivers: their chance (percent) to fire it at a car ahead
    ai_special_behind = 1..100      ... and at a car behind (else the special's own habits)
The numbers apply to whatever special the car fires, its own or the one chosen. A mass of 100 doubles the damage of
every special a player fires (Dark Tooth's rule), so keep mass under 100.

And:
    radar_color = r, g, b       its dot on the radar, 0..255 each (else the base car's)

The name over a targeted car is the character's name in capital letters (letters, digits and spaces).


Team colors
-----------
In team games the game repaints part of each car in its team's color (red, blue, green or yellow): some palette
entries of the car's body texture, the first picture in its TMS, shifted to the team's color, while the windows,
tires, weapons and driver keep theirs. List your car's paint entries in mod.ini, under [character]:

    team_paint = 16-47, 80, 82-95     ; palette entries 0..255 of car.tms's first picture

Keep the paint in palette entries of its own (not shared with the glass or the tires), so only the paint turns.
Without team_paint the car keeps its own colors in team games.


The computer driver
-------------------
A computer car on your character drives with the base car's habits, changed by these keys under [ai] (each optional;
the cars' own values in the brackets). Every so often the driver picks a mood: hunt (chase its target), attack, or
else flee. The chances blend from the full-health ones to the hurt ones as it loses health; hunt and attack together
are at most 100, and what is left of 100 is fleeing.

    hunt = 0..100           its chance to hunt at full health (20 Outlaw 2 .. 100 Hammerhead, Mr. Slam)
    hunt_hurt = 0..100      ... with no health left (0 .. 100)
    attack = 0..100         its chance to attack at full health (0 .. 80 Outlaw 2)
    attack_hurt = 0..100    ... with no health left (0 .. 100)
    hunt_time = 150..3600   how long a hunt lasts, in ticks (300 .. 3600; most cars 600)
    attack_time = 150..3600 how long an attack lasts (300 .. 1800)
    flee_time = 150..3600   how long it flees (300 .. 600)
    retaliate = 0..100      how much of its health one car may take before it turns on that car, percent (7 .. 100)
    pass = 0..100           its chance to try to pass a car in its way (0 .. 90)
    patience = 10..200      how long a target may pull away before it plans a new way to it (12 .. 180)
    fire_chance = 1..100    how often it fires its weapons (the base car's own when left out)

ai_special_ahead and ai_special_behind under [character] (see "The special") are its chance to fire its special.


Sounds
------
engine.wav replaces the base car's engine and special.wav the sound of its special weapon. Each is optional: without
it the car has its base's sound.

    WAV, PCM, 8 or 16 bits, mono or stereo (mixed to mono), 8000 to 48000 Hz
    engine.wav      at most 4 seconds: the engine at idle, made to loop (its end runs into its start). The game
                    raises its pitch as the car speeds up, as it does its own engines'.
    special.wav     at most 6 seconds. It loops when the base car's special sound loops (Thumper's flame), else it
                    plays once each time.

The game plays them through its own sound chip's voices: its volume, distance, echo and pitch apply as to its own.


The ending
----------
Winning the tournament with your character on Medium or Hard earns its ending in that player's profile. Earned
endings are in Options > Movies > Mods. A character's win never unlocks a game driver's ending, nor what those
unlock.

An ending can be a video, a page, or both:
- ending.mpg: a video, played first, with its sound. A button skips it after half a second.
- The page: ending.bmp (else its portrait), "<NAME> WINS", and the text of ending.txt (a few short paragraphs; a
  blank line between them). With a video, the page follows only when there is an ending.txt.
- Without either, the page shows its portrait and "<NAME> WINS".

The video
---------
ending.mpg must be MPEG-1 video with MP2 sound, in an MPEG file (.mpg). This is not the same as an .mp4, .mkv or
.avi, even renamed: those hold other kinds of video the game cannot play. Convert yours with ffmpeg (free,
ffmpeg.org), from any video it can read:

    ffmpeg -i my_ending.mp4 -c:v mpeg1video -b:v 2000k -c:a mp2 -b:a 192k -s 640x480 ending.mpg

    -b:v 2000k      the picture's data rate: about 16 MB a minute, sharper than the game's own movies
    -s 640x480      the size: 640 x 480 (4:3), or 854 x 480 for a 16:9 video
    no sound        leave out "-c:a mp2 -b:a 192k" and add -an

ffmpeg may say "VBV buffer size not set, using default size": that is fine.

The limits:
    picture         MPEG-1 (not MPEG-2: ffmpeg's -c:v mpeg1video, not mpeg2video), at most 854 x 480
    sound           MP2 (not MP3 or AAC), or none
    length          at most 2 minutes
    file            at most 40 MB (2 minutes with the command above: about 33 MB)

tm2pc.exe --check-mod tells you when the video breaks a rule ("is MPEG-2 video", "has sound that is not MP2",
"is 1280 x 720", ...). A video that breaks one is never played (the page is shown instead).


Online
------
A server uses the host's characters that are on, as with skins: joining asks to download the ones you lack.
