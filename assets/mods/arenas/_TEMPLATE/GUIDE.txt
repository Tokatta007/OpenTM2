Arenas: a new map
=================

Status: the game loads and plays arena mods. An arena mod that is on (Options > Mods) is listed after the secret
arenas in every arena list, and Random can pick it. Online, players joining a server download its mods first. The
formats of the level files are still being written down.


Files
-----
    mod.ini        the manifest (../../README.txt), kind = arena
    arena.dmd      geometry, objects and textures' use, in the game's level format (as the disc's DMD\<level>.DMD);
                   pickups, breakables and pedestrians are objects in it
    arena.tms      the textures (as TMS\<level>.TMS)
    arena.grp      the sections (floors, edges) and the computer cars' route tables (as TERRAIN\<level>.GRP)
    arena.rmp      ramps and raised surfaces (as TERRAIN\<level>.RMP)
    arena.pts      the computer cars' route points (below; as TERRAIN\<level>.PTS)
    arena.ini      name, route and section counts, start points, sky color, teleporters, the hidden health spot,
                   fall heights (below)
    card.bmp       its level-select card: 344 x 260, its name on it (required)
    lineup.bmp     the picture behind the opponents' cards before a match: 320 x 240 or larger, 4:3 (required)
    wide.bmp       a 2:1 picture for the brick frame (Survival): 512 x 256 (optional: made from the card)
    music.wav / .mp3 / .flac / .ogg   its own music (optional: else a random arena's track from the game)
    radar.bmp      (optional, for later)

The picture frames: mods\arenas\_TEMPLATE gets card_template.png and wide_template.png from your own disc's art
(the game's tools make them); draw the scene under the frame and save as card.bmp / wide.bmp.


What the level files hold
-------------------------
- arena.dmd: everything you see and hit. Besides the arena's own meshes it must carry the shared objects every
  level's DMD has (explosions and effects, the dashboard icons, the radar arrows, car and weapon parts): start from
  a disc level's DMD and keep those objects. Its pointers are for the address the game loads levels at, as the
  disc's are.
- Objects in the DMD the game treats by number: pickups (401-430, at most 50 in a level), breakables (500-520),
  teleporter pads (693-697, at most 3: below). Don't use target 510: it is Holland's barn, and destroying one switches
  off parts of the map (Holland's sections), whatever arena it is in.
- Room: the level's DMD shares the game's level memory (about 720 KB) with the cars' models; the disc's levels are
  300 to 540 KB, so keep a mod's DMD in that range. The TMS images go where each TIM says in video memory, as the
  disc's levels do: keep the places a disc level leaves free for the cars, the shadows and the turbo flame. The
  bosses need room too: Dark Tooth (Survival's last round, a custom tournament's boss) has many textures, and on an
  arena whose textures leave him no room, Minion comes in his place (as on Paris).
- arena.grp: at most 35 sections, and the tables that join them (below). arena.rmp: at most 225 surfaces.
- The radar shows only the cars (the game has no map picture): radar.bmp is for the port's own radar, later.


Teleporters
-----------
A teleporter is a pad object in arena.dmd (693-697) and a line in arena.ini. An arena without warp lines has no
teleporters. Up to 3:

    warp1 = x, y, z, x, y, z, 2  ; the pad's place, where it sends a car, and its pair (0 to 2)
    warp1_exit = 46, 9           ; a computer car leaving it: the route point it comes out at, the one it heads for

The pad's place and the place it sends a car are the game's coordinates (x, y the ground, z the height), as the
route points'. Without a warpN_exit line computer cars don't come out of that teleporter in a planned place. The
copy of Holland that tools/mods/clone_arena.py makes has Holland's own lines, as an example.


The hidden health spot
----------------------
    health_spot = -1168, -6200   ; the low corner of a 64 x 64 square on the ground (x, y)

A player's car that stops inside the square gets its health back, once a match. Without the line the arena has
none. (Holland's is the example above.)


Pickups
-------
Yes, an arena needs its own pickups: the game has no pickup list anywhere but in the level's DMD. Each pickup is an
object placed in arena.dmd, and its object number says what it is (401-430: the weapons, turbo, health and the
rest, as in the disc's levels). At most 50 in a level. Spread weapons and health through every part of the arena,
and put a few near the start points. A game type's Weapons and pickups settings then work on them as on the
game's arenas. Copy the placements' look from a disc level of a similar size.


Start points and respawns
-------------------------
The start points (route points 0..14, listed in arena.ini) are also where cars come back in the respawn modes
(Deathmatch, Juggernaut, Gun Game, Hunted, King of the Hill). A match can have up to 16 cars: give all 15, spread
far apart and facing open ground, never at an edge. With fewer, cars land on each other (Los Angeles has 10), and the
port then picks the clearest route point instead.


Routes for computer cars
------------------------
Without route points, computer cars cannot drive an arena. The game's limits:

- at most 140 route points and 35 sections;
- up to 15 start points (route points 0..14);
- each point links to up to 4 others, each link with a lane byte that sets its top speed
  (-2 a quarter, -1 a third, 0 half, 1 two thirds, 2 full speed);
- two tables join the sections: next node [from section][to section] (the exit point to drive to) and
  next section [from section][to section].

How a computer car uses them: in its target's section it chases the target; anywhere else it drives to the exit
point toward the target's section, then on into the next section. At each point it takes the link nearest where it
is going. So lay out every section so that heading for any of its points works from anywhere in it.

What worked for the port's own routes (Cyburbia, Roof Tops):

- one junction point per fork; thin out points on curves;
- links both ways unless a one-way drop is really meant;
- keep points at least 700 units from any edge a car can fall from, and no dead-end points near one;
- cap a link's speed by how much ground is left past its end;
- jumps as New York's: a run-up point about 900 before the edge and a landing point about 850 past the far edge,
  and the jump's speed in the speed table [from section][to section];
- point 0 should not be in a closed-off spot: Dark Tooth's warp can send cars to point 0 on any level;
- check every trip: from each point to each other point, the walk above must arrive. The port's generators
  (tools/routes/cyburbia.py, roof_tops.py in its source) each include such a check; a mod checker will run the same
  walk.


arena.ini
---------
The game sets these per level in its code; a mod arena gives them here. The game reads these now:

    [arena]
    name = Suburbs              ; the level select, the line-up
    sky = 40, 60, 120           ; the background color (r, g, b)
    points = 58                 ; the route points in arena.pts (1 to 140)
    starts = 0, 2, 13, 15       ; the start points: route points, up to 15
    sections = 7                ; the sections in arena.grp (1 to 35)
    surfaces = 8                ; the surfaces in arena.rmp (0 to 225)
    short_chase = 1             ; 1: computer cars chase from nearer (small arenas), 0: from farther (big ones)
    warp1 = x, y, z, x, y, z, 1 ; a teleporter: its pad, where it sends a car, its pair (0 to 2); up to 3
                                ; (no warp lines: no teleporters)
    fall_limit = -8000          ; how far below the floor a car may fall before it is lost (optional)
    fall_death = 80             ; the height under which a car is destroyed (optional)
    health_spot = x, y          ; the hidden health (above; optional: none without it)
    warp1_exit = a, b           ; the computer cars' way out of teleporter 1 (above)

Music: put music.wav, music.mp3, music.flac or music.ogg in the mod's folder and it plays in this arena. Without one,
a random arena's track from the game plays. Players can set it themselves in Options > Custom Music (the "Mod
arenas" row).

Planned (not read yet):

    hill1 = x, y, z             ; King of the Hill hills (5 to 8)

Where the game's code has per-level rules that no line above sets (the computer cars' strength, for one), a mod
arena plays by Holland's.

To start from one of your own disc's arenas: python tools/mods/clone_arena.py <your disc's track 1 .bin> 7
mods/arenas/<id> makes a copy of Holland as a mod (its files from your disc: never share them).

Arena events that are code in the original game (Los Angeles's quakes, Moscow's collapse, the Eiffel tower, Hong
Kong's trains, New York's and Holland's targets) cannot come from a mod. Breakables and pedestrians, which are
objects in the DMD, can.
