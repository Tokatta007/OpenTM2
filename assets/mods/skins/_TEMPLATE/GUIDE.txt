Skins: a new look for an existing car
=====================================

A skin replaces how one of the game's cars looks in a match: its model and its textures. Everything else stays that
car's: its stats, its special weapon, its sounds and its hit box. So a skin never changes how the car plays, and it
cannot make a car stronger.


Making one
----------
1. Run tm2pc.exe --new-mod skin spectre-gold: it makes mods\skins\spectre-gold with a mod.ini, its id and a new
   uid filled in. (Or copy this _TEMPLATE folder there and make a uid yourself: ../../README.txt.)
2. Edit mod.ini: your name for it, your name, and the car it is for (see "The cars" below).
3. Add the two files below.
4. Run tm2pc.exe --check-mod mods\skins\spectre-gold until it prints OK.
5. Start the game. In car select, highlight its car and press Select: each press shows the car's next look (its
   own, then each of its skins that is on). Options > Mods turns each skin on or off.


Files
-----
    mod.ini         the manifest (../../README.txt) and [skin] car = <the car>
    car.dmd         the car's model, in the game's own format (as the disc's DMD\<code>.DMD)
    car.tms         the car's textures, in the game's own format (as the disc's TMS\<code>.TMS)

Optional, as a pair:
    carselect.dmd, carselect.tms     the car turning in car select (without them, car.dmd / car.tms)

Optional pictures (BMP; without them the car's own are shown):
    portrait_1.bmp   the driver's portrait in car select: 64 x 128, or larger in the same shape (128 x 256 ...)
    portrait_2.bmp   the same once the car is chosen (the game shows a second portrait)
    carinfo.bmp      the CAR INFO screen (the car's ratings and story): 640 x 480
    plate.bmp        the car's picture in the match line-up and the lobby: 128 x 64, or larger in the same shape

Both car.dmd and car.tms are needed. A skin that only recolors the car still ships the car's own car.dmd beside its
new car.tms.


Recoloring a car (the easiest skin)
-----------------------------------
A car's TMS file is a pack of PlayStation TIM images, each with its palettes (CLUTs). Changing the palette colors
recolors the car without touching its shape:

- Take the car's DMD and TMS from your own disc (DMD\SP.DMD and TMS\SP.TMS for Spectre).
- Edit the palettes of the TIM images inside the TMS with a TIM editor, keeping each image's size, bit depth and
  place. Color 0 (all zero) is see-through: keep it.
- Save the result as car.tms, and the unchanged DMD as car.dmd.

The game's file codes: HH Hammerhead, OT Outlaw 2, WH Warthog, MG Mr. Grimm, PV Grasshopper, TH Thumper,
SP Spectre, RK Roadkill, IR Twister, AX Axel, FL Mr. Slam, HS Shadow, ST Sweet Tooth, MN Minion, BB Dark Tooth.

Team colors
-----------
In team games the game repaints part of each car in its team's color (red, blue, green or yellow): some palette
entries of the car's body texture, the first picture in its TMS, shifted to the team's color, while the windows,
tires, weapons and driver keep theirs. List your car's paint entries in mod.ini, under [skin]:

    team_paint = 16-47, 80, 82-95     ; palette entries 0..255 of car.tms's first picture

Keep the paint in palette entries of its own (not shared with the glass or the tires), so only the paint turns.
Without team_paint the car keeps its own colors in team games.


A new model
-----------
A different body is possible, with limits:

- The game finds parts of a car's model by number (the special weapon's objects, moving parts such as Axel's
  wheels or Mr. Slam's arm, the damage looks). Start from the car's own DMD and change its meshes, keeping its node
  tree and numbers, or those parts go missing.
- Keep it about the same size as the original (within about 15%). The hit box stays the original car's, so a much
  bigger body would be hit through and a much smaller one would be hit by nothing.
- A car with its own size, stats or special is a character, not a skin (../../characters/_TEMPLATE/GUIDE.txt).

A converter from common 3D formats is planned; until then a new model needs a tool that writes the game's DMD format.


The cars
--------
[skin] car = one of:
    Hammerhead, Outlaw 2, Warthog, Mr. Grimm, Grasshopper, Thumper, Spectre, Roadkill, Twister, Axel, Mr. Slam,
    Shadow, Sweet Tooth, Minion, Dark Tooth


Limits now
----------
- Online, a server uses the host's skins that are on; joining asks to download the ones you lack.
- Every player picks with Select, in every car select. Computer cars always use the original look.
- On a Playlist server, the vote screen between matches has each player's car: Up and Down step through its skins
  (only the ones the server has, so everyone can load them).
- Up to 128 skins can be installed. Each match loads only the skins its players picked.
